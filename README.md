# Older-Adult Fall Deaths by State, 2023 — MyElderHome dataset

This repository contains the dataset behind the MyElderHome study "Older-Adult Fall Deaths by State: What the CDC's 2023 Numbers Show".

## Data file

- `older-adult-falls-by-state-2023.csv` — state-by-state fall deaths and rates for adults age 65 and older in 2023. Columns: `state`, `fall_deaths`, `rate_per_100k`.

## Source

- CDC NCHS Data Brief No. 532 (2023 state rates): Garnett MF, Weeks JD, Zehner AM. "Unintentional Fall Deaths in Adults Age 65 and Older: United States, 2023," NCHS Data Brief No. 532 (June 2025), National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS).

## Study page

## Open Science Framework

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/9CD7K

## Notes

- Rates are as published by CDC. They are crude rates — deaths per 100,000 residents age 65 and older — exactly as published in NCHS Data Brief No. 532.
- The table includes the United States total (41,400 fall deaths; 69.9 per 100,000) followed by the 50 states and the District of Columbia.
